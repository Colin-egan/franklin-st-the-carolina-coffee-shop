About Us

North Carolina's oldest continuously operating restaurant

Our Story

Established in 1922, the Carolina Coffee Shop is the oldest continuously operating restaurant in North Carolina. From morning espresso in our historic booths to weekend brunch and evening cocktails, we've been a Franklin Street gathering place for over a century.
